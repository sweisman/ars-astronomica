Bartolomeo Spina was a Dominican theologian from Pisa. He rose through the order's
schools, and in 1542 Paul III made him Master of the Sacred Palace, the pope's own
theologian. His *Quaestio de strigibus*, printed at Venice in 1523, came out of the witch
trials of northern Italy. It is followed by four replies to the jurist Giovanni Francesco
Ponzinibio, who had argued that the witches' flight was a delusion and that their
confessions proved nothing. Spina holds the opposite on every point, as [Nicolas
Jacquier](/authors/nicolas-jacquier/) had two generations earlier.
