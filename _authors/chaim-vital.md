R. Chaim Vital was born in Safed and is remembered as the foremost disciple of R. Isaac
Luria, the Ari. Luria taught in Safed for barely two years before his death in 1572 and
wrote almost nothing; what is known of his system comes largely through Vital's record of
it, above all the *Etz Chaim*. Vital spent his last two decades in Damascus, where he
died. *Sefer HaTechunah* shows another side of him: a treatise on the astronomy of the
spheres, worked out in the ordinary technical vocabulary of the science.

## In the corpus
