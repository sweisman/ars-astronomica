David Origanus, born David Tost in Glatz in Silesia, taught Greek and mathematics at the
Viadrina University in Frankfurt an der Oder. He is known for his ephemerides, the tables
of daily planetary positions on which calendar-makers, navigators and astrologers
depended. Their long introduction is a textbook of astronomy in its own right. It is
notable for accepting the daily rotation of the Earth while keeping the Earth at the
centre: the arrangement of [Tycho Brahe](/authors/tycho-brahe/), with a spinning Earth.

## In the corpus

- [*Ephemerides Brandenburgicae*](/works/david-origanus-ephemerides-brandenburgicae/) (1609). Sixty years of daily positions of the Sun, Moon and planets, computed on both Tychonic and Copernican foundations. [PDF](https://books.arsastronomica.com/David_Origanus_Ephemerides_Brandenburgicae.pdf)
