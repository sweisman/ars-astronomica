Ernesto Bozzano of Genoa was among the most prolific psychical researchers of his
generation. Self-taught, and at first a convinced positivist, he turned to the subject in
the 1890s and gave the rest of his life to it. His method was the monograph: for each kind
of reported phenomenon he gathered the published cases, sorted them into classes, and
argued from the classification. *Phénomènes psychiques au moment de la mort* applies that
method to what is reported at deathbeds.

## In the corpus

- [*Phénomènes psychiques au moment de la mort*](/works/ernesto-bozzano-phenomenes-psychiques-au-moment-de-la-mort/) (1923). Three studies classifying the phenomena reported at deathbeds: apparitions of the dead, objects moving at the moment of death, and music no one played.
