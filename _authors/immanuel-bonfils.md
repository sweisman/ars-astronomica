R. Immanuel ben Jacob Bonfils lived at Tarascon in Provence in the middle of the
fourteenth century. His *Shesh Kenafayim*, the Six Wings, composed around 1365, became one
of the most widely used Hebrew astronomical handbooks: it was translated into Latin and
into Byzantine Greek, and was still being consulted centuries later. A short note of his
sets out decimal fractions and their arithmetic explicitly, some two hundred years before
Simon Stevin made them common in Europe.

## In the corpus

- [*Shesh Kenafayim*](/works/immanuel-bonfils-shesh-kenafayim/) (c. 1365). Six sets of tables for the Sun and Moon, eclipses and calendars, using decimal fractions long before Europe adopted them. [PDF](https://books.arsastronomica.com/Immanuel_Bonfils_Shesh_Kenafayim.pdf)
