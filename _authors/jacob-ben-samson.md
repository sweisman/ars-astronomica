R. Jacob ben Samson was a scholar of northern France in the early twelfth century, a pupil
of Rashi and, by tradition, a teacher of Rashi's grandson Rabbenu Tam. Little of his
writing survives under his own name. He is credited with a commentary on tractate *Avot*
and with a work on the calendar dated 1123. The *Perush Sod HaIbbur* translated here,
preserved in the North French Hebrew Miscellany, is attributed to him rather than signed,
and the work's own page marks it so.

## In the corpus

- [*Perush Sod HaIbbur*](/works/jacob-ben-samson-perush-sod-haibbur/) (c. 1123). An early Ashkenazi commentary on computing the Hebrew calendar, from Rashi's circle, preserved in the North French Hebrew Miscellany.
