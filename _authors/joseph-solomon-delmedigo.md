Joseph Solomon Delmedigo was born in Candia, on Crete, and studied medicine at Padua,
where he heard Galileo lecture and looked through his telescope. He then spent his life on
the move as a physician: Cairo, Constantinople, the Karaite communities of Lithuania,
Hamburg, Amsterdam, Frankfurt and finally Prague, where he died. *Sefer Elim*, printed at
Amsterdam in 1629 by Menasseh ben Israel, is framed as a reply to questions from the
Karaite scholar Zerah ben Nathan, and ranges across mathematics, astronomy, physics and
metaphysics. It is one of the first Hebrew books to side with Copernicus.
