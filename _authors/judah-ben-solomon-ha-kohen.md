R. Judah ben Solomon ha-Kohen, also called Ibn Matkah, was born in Toledo and studied
there under R. Meir Abulafia. As a young man he exchanged questions on geometry and
astronomy with a philosopher at the court of the emperor Frederick II, and later travelled
to Italy, to the court itself. There, around 1247, he rendered into Hebrew the
encyclopedia he had first written in Arabic. *Midrash HaḤokhmah* surveys logic, natural
science, mathematics and astronomy, and is among the first works of its kind in Hebrew.

## In the corpus

- [*Midrash HaḤokhmah*](/works/judah-ben-solomon-ha-kohen-midrash-hahokhmah/) (c. 1247). An encyclopedia of logic, natural science and astronomy, translated by its author from his own Arabic for Hebrew readers.
