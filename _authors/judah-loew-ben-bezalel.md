R. Judah Loew ben Bezalel, the Maharal of Prague, served as rabbi in Mikulov in Moravia,
in Poznań and above all in Prague, where he founded a school and where he died in 1609.
His books form a connected body of thought on the Torah, the nature of Israel, exile and
redemption, and on how the sayings of the Talmudic sages are to be read. He argued for
reform in Jewish education, and counted the astronomer [David Gans](/authors/david-gans/)
among his students. *Be'er HaGolah*, printed in 1598, defends the Aggadah against critics
who found its stories absurd and its science out of date.

## In the corpus

- [*Be'er HaGolah*](/works/judah-loew-ben-bezalel-beer-hagolah/) (1598). A defence of the Aggadah against critics who read its stories as absurd and its science as discredited, in seven 'wells' sunk toward one spring. [PDF](https://books.arsastronomica.com/Judah_Loew_ben_Bezalel_Beer_HaGolah.pdf)
