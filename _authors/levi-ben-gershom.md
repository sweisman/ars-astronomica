R. Levi ben Gershom, known as Gersonides or by the acronym Ralbag, lived in Provence, at
Bagnols and Orange, and was one of the boldest minds of the Jewish Middle Ages. He wrote
commentaries on the Bible and on Averroes, treatises on logic and mathematics, and the
*Wars of the Lord*, completed in 1329, which takes up the questions where Aristotle and
the Torah collide. He was also a working astronomer: he observed for decades, invented the
instrument later called the Jacob's staff, and rejected parts of Ptolemy's system where
his measurements contradicted it. His astronomy forms Book V, Part 1 of the *Wars* and was
never printed.
