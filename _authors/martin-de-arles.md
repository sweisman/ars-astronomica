Martín de Arles y Andosilla was a professor of theology, a canon of Pamplona cathedral and
archdeacon of the valley of Aibar in Navarre. His *Tractatus de superstitionibus* was
written from what he met among the people in his charge: the charms, lucky and unlucky
days, weather rites and divinations of the countryside, each weighed against the teaching
of the Church. On the flight of witches he follows the old Canon Episcopi and treats it as
a delusion, which sets him apart from the inquisitorial writers elsewhere in this corpus,
[Nicolas Jacquier](/authors/nicolas-jacquier/) and [Bartolomeo
Spina](/authors/bartolomeo-spina/).
