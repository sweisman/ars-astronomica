R. Mordecai ben Eliezer Comtino was one of the leading Rabbanite scholars of
Constantinople in the fifteenth century, in the years on either side of the Ottoman
conquest. He wrote commentaries on the Pentateuch and on works of [Avraham ibn
Ezra](/authors/avraham-ibn-ezra/), and treatises on arithmetic, geometry and astronomical
instruments. He was unusual in teaching Karaite students as well as Rabbanite ones, and
several of the next generation's scholars on both sides were his pupils, among them
[Eliyahu Mizrahi](/authors/eliyahu-mizrahi/). The *Sefer HaḤeshbon VeHaMiddot* is his
textbook of arithmetic and practical geometry.
