Nicolas Jacquier was a Dominican who attended the Council of Basel and afterwards served
as an inquisitor in northern France and the Burgundian Netherlands, where he sat on the
Arras *vauderie* trials. His *Flagellum haereticorum fascinariorum*, written in 1458,
argues against the Canon Episcopi that the witches' gatherings are no dream: they happen
in the body, in waking life, and those who attend are heretics. It is the first sustained
inquisitorial defence of that position, written a generation before the *Malleus
Maleficarum*.
