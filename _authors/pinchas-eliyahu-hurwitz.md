R. Pinchas Eliyahu Hurwitz was born in Vilna and spent years travelling, through Galicia,
Germany, Holland and England, gathering the material for a single book. *Sefer HaBris* was
printed anonymously at Brünn in 1797 and became one of the most widely read Hebrew books
of its era, reprinted many times and translated into Yiddish and Ladino. Its first part is
an encyclopedia of the natural sciences as he had learned them; its second is a guide to
the spiritual life drawn from Lurianic Kabbalah. He presented the whole as a preparation
for a work of [Chaim Vital](/authors/chaim-vital/), whose astronomy is also in this
corpus.
