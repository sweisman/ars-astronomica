Thomas Erastus, born Thomas Lieber at Baden in Switzerland, studied theology at Basel and
medicine at Bologna and Padua, and from 1558 was professor of medicine at Heidelberg and
physician to the Elector Palatine. He wrote at length against Paracelsus and against
astrology. In church politics he argued that discipline belonged to the civil magistrate
and not to the clergy, the position later called Erastianism. His *De lamiis*, printed at
Basel in 1578, answers Johann Weyer: Erastus grants that witches exist and deserve death,
then denies on medical and physical grounds nearly every marvel attributed to them.
