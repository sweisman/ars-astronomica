Ernesto Bozzano (1862–1943) of Genoa was among the most prolific psychical
researchers of his generation. *Phénomènes psychiques au moment de la mort* is a
methodical study of the phenomena reported at and around the moment of dying. It
collects cases from the published spiritist and psychical-research literature,
classifies them by type, and argues from the classification.

## What is in this book

The book brings together three of Bozzano's monographs:

- **On apparitions of the deceased at the deathbed**: phantoms of the dead
  perceived by the dying, and in some cases by those around them.
- **On phenomena of "telekinesis" in connection with deaths**: objects moving,
  falling or sounding at or near the time of a death.
- **Transcendental music**: music and sounds reported at deathbeds, in
  hauntings, through mediums, and after a death. This is the part of the book
  with the fewest parallels elsewhere in the literature.

Each monograph sets its cases out in categories of increasing complexity. Bozzano
comments on each case and draws his conclusions at the end.

## Why it matters

Bozzano states his method plainly. He grants that many of these experiences can
be explained as hallucination, and he argues that a classification of the whole
body of cases leaves a remainder that hallucination and telepathy do not cover.
He concludes that consciousness survives the death of the body. Whatever a
reader makes of that conclusion, the book is a primary document of the
psychical research of the early twentieth century, and of its attempt to bring
the methods of the natural sciences to the question of survival.
