The *Coeli et siderum in eo errantium Observationes Hassiacae* is the first
printing of the observations made at Kassel under Landgrave Wilhelm IV of Hesse.
Willebrord Snellius edited it and dedicated it to Wilhelm's son Maurice.

## What is in this book

The Kassel observations of the planets come first. The book adds a selection
from Tycho Brahe's Bohemian observations and, under its own signatures, the
Nuremberg observations of Regiomontanus and Bernhard Walther. Around them are
Johann Schöner's notes on the cross-staff, tables, a passage from Kepler on
Walther's refraction, and Regiomontanus's sixteen problems on the size and
distance of comets. Snellius addresses the reader directly, comparing the
results.

## Why it matters

Together with Tycho's own records, the Kassel series is the chief body of
precision observation from before the telescope.

## About this edition

The Bavarian State Library copy ends at printed page 116, although its title page
announces the Nuremberg observations, so this edition takes that second sequence
from the copy in the ETH-Bibliothek Zürich. Snellius's own
[*Eratosthenes Batavus*](/works/willebrord-snellius-eratosthenes-batavus/) is
also in this corpus.
